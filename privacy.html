Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

Daily Health Dashboard is a personal, local-first application for viewing health, activity, sleep, training, and meal information. It is operated by Mayuresh Budukh for personal use.

Information handled by the application

Depending on the features you use, the application may handle health and fitness records (including sleep, readiness, activity, workouts, and heart-rate data), meal notes, training information, and the dates and identifiers needed to organize those records. Provider connection credentials and tokens may also be stored by the local Open Wearables service to maintain an authorized connection.

How information is used

Information is used only to populate and operate the personal dashboard, maintain provider synchronization, and create user-requested exports or backups. It is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally shared with other people by this application.

Where information is stored and transmitted

In the current personal setup, dashboard records are stored in a SQLite database on the operator's Mac. Provider data is retrieved through a locally operated Open Wearables service and stored by that service in its local database. The service may send authorization requests and data requests to the relevant provider, such as Oura, and receives data in response. Those providers handle information under their own privacy terms.

The local services may be reachable by devices on the operator's private home network. Network traffic on that network may not be encrypted. This setup is not intended to be exposed to the public internet. Backups or exports created by the operator may be stored separately and are subject to the operator's control.

Retention and deletion

Information remains in local databases and backups until the operator deletes it. The operator can stop provider synchronization by disconnecting or revoking the provider authorization and can remove locally stored records by deleting the relevant local application data. Deleting one copy does not automatically delete separate exports or backups, or data held by a provider.

Security

The application is designed for local personal use, but no computer or network storage can be guaranteed completely secure. The operator is responsible for securing the Mac, its accounts, credentials, local network, backups, and any devices that can access the services.

Children

This application is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

This notice may be updated to reflect changes in the application or its operation. For privacy questions or requests, contact Mayuresh Budukh on GitHub.
